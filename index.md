# ORYUNE Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 7, 2026**

ORYUNE respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ORYUNE mobile game.

## Information We Collect

ORYUNE does not require you to create an account.

Game progress, settings, unlocked Spirits, scores, challenges, and other gameplay progress are primarily stored locally on your device.

ORYUNE uses third-party services that may collect or process certain information as described below.

## Advertising

ORYUNE uses Google Mobile Ads (Google AdMob) to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, and information related to ad interactions. The information processed may depend on your device settings, location, and consent choices.

Where required by applicable law, ORYUNE uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent before advertising data is processed.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please see Google's Privacy & Terms documentation.

## In-App Purchases

ORYUNE offers an optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. ORYUNE does not receive or store your payment card or banking information.

Purchase entitlement information may be processed through Apple's StoreKit services so that purchases can be completed and restored.

## Game Data

ORYUNE stores gameplay information locally on your device, including information such as:

- High scores
- Progress and experience
- Unlocked Spirits
- Challenge progress
- Game settings
- Purchase entitlement state

This information is used to provide and maintain the game's functionality.

## Consent and Advertising Choices

In regions where consent is required, you may be presented with options regarding advertising and data processing.

Your available choices may depend on your location and applicable privacy regulations.

You can also manage certain privacy and advertising settings through your device settings.

## Children's Privacy

ORYUNE is not specifically directed at children under the age required to consent to the processing of personal data in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to ORYUNE.

## Third-Party Services

ORYUNE uses services provided by:

- Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
- Google User Messaging Platform
- Apple App Store / StoreKit

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ORYUNE changes, when third-party services change, or when required by applicable law.

The latest version will be available on this page.
