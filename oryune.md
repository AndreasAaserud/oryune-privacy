# ORYUNE

**Flip gravity. Find your flow.**

ORYUNE is a minimalist arcade game for iPhone built around one simple action: tap to flip gravity.

Navigate a dreamlike world, pass through gates, build your Flow with precise timing, discover Rifts, and unlock new Spirits as you progress.

## Features

- Simple one-tap gameplay
- Skill-based scoring
- Perfect gates and Flow streaks
- Rare Rift bonuses
- Unlockable Spirits
- Challenges and progression
- Spirit Echo of your previous best
- Optional Remove Ads purchase
