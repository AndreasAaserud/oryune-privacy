## In-App Purchases

If you previously purchased Remove Ads and need to restore your purchase, open ORYUNE and select **Restore Purchases** from the main menu.

Purchases are associated with your Apple Account and are processed by Apple through the App Store.

## Advertising

ORYUNE may display advertisements. An optional Remove Ads purchase is available from the main menu.

## Reporting a Problem

When contacting support, please include:

- A short description of the problem
- Your iPhone model
- Your iOS version
- The version of ORYUNE you are using

Please do not include passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information.
